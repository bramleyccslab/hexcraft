Instructions (Click to hide)

You are a one of a team of scientists working on synthetic protein folding.

Your shared goal is to find a way to replicate target proteins (shown below as green shapes).

There are 9 basic chemical operations you can use to achieve this:

  • a Adds a single piece of matter in the center of the board
  • d Removes any piece of matter in the center of the board
  • c Adds three pieces of matter in a corner shape
  • b Adds three pieces of matter in a bar shape
  • r Reflects all pieces horizontally, duplicating them
  • o Rotates all pieces 60 degrees clockwise
  • w,e,s Shift all pieces one space to the West, North-East, and South-East, respectively.
  1. Your additions can overlap existing additions.
  2. Your transformation actions (moving, and rotating and reflecting) affect everything on the board.
  3. Any pieces that fall off the edge of the board are lost.
  4. You succeed by covering the green pattern exactly.
  5. Your the current state of your synthetic protein is shown in gray.
  6. Use the red dot to track how the board rotates.

Working together:

  1. Everyone in the team takes turns to contribute actions.
  2. To be eligible for bonus points, you must contribute at least one action per protein.
  3. Click Act if you are ready to contribute one or more actions to the current attempt.
  4. Click Reset if you make a mistake and want to clear the actions you have typed.
  5. If you do not want to add anything, press Pass.
  6. If you want to start over, press Vote Restart. A new attempt will be started if everyone votes Restart.
